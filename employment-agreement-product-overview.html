Employment lifecycle

Core employment terms

  • ✓Position and duties. The employee performs duties associated with their title and follows company direction and policies.
  • ✓Time commitment. The employee devotes substantially all business time and attention to company work, subject to permitted leave.
  • ✓Compensation and benefits. Salary, PTO, holidays, sick time, and other standard benefits are addressed.
  • ✓Policy alignment. The agreement controls if it conflicts with general company policies.
Separation & protection

At-will, with a clean exit

  • ✓At-will status. Either party may end the relationship at any time, with or without cause, subject to applicable law.
  • ✓Notice period. The template supports a selected number of written-notice days for either party.
  • ✓Continued pay. If the company requests work during notice, regular compensation continues through the termination date.
  • ✓Property return. The employee returns all company property and retains no copies or notes.
