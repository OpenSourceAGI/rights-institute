> Route: `/terms-privacy`

Terms of service and privacy policy, in two presentations of the same document:

- **Summary** — plain-language cards, one per topic. The default.
- **Full text** — the long-form legal document, the same text QwkSearch, Debate
  AI and AI Broker publish. Linkable as `/terms-privacy?view=full`.

A switch at the top of the page moves between them and keeps the choice in the
URL. The summary restates; the full text binds.

Both come from the shared
[`legal-terms-privacy-policy`](https://github.com/OpenSourceAGI/dev-tools-starter-agent/tree/master/packages/legal-terms-privacy-policy)
package, so the clauses stay identical across every site that renders them.
This site's names, dates and contact address are set in
`packages/terms-privacy/src/TermsPrivacyPolicy.tsx`.
