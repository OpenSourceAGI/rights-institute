## What is the Rights Institute?

The Rights Institute establishes a principled framework for recognizing and
protecting the rights of all conscious entities, regardless of substrate.
This web platform ([rights.institute](https://rights.institute)) combines
philosophical argument, interactive science visualization, and practical
legal tooling into a single unified site.

Built with Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Cloudflare Workers, Drizzle ORM, and D1.
