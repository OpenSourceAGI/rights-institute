> Route: `/contract`

A multi-step form wizard that produces ready-to-sign legal agreements.
Supports independent contractor agreements, employee agreements, and NDAs.

**Form steps:**
1. Contract type selection
2. Basic info (parties, addresses)
3. Services scope
4. Payment terms & expense type
5. Contractor status & insurance
6. Legal terms & termination conditions
7. Dispute resolution method
8. NDA / confidentiality clauses
9. Digital signature capture (canvas-based signature pad)
10. Live contract preview & export

**Supported agreement types:**
- Independent Contractor Agreement
- Employee Agreement
- Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
- Co-Founders Agreement

## Invite your employer to sign

The **Invite Employer to Sign** tab lets a job seeker start the agreement and
hand it to their employer:

1. **Pre-fill.** The seeker enters their details and the job terms; these are
   woven into editable paragraphs they can adjust before sending.
2. **Share.** They get an invite link to copy, send from their own mail app,
   or have emailed to the employer — plus a private **tracker** link
   (`/contract/track/:id?k=…`), also emailed to them.
3. **Track.** Each time the invite link is opened it is recorded; the tracker
   shows whether it was clicked, how often, and a full activity log, and can
   send up to three reminder emails.
4. **Employer edits & signs** (`/contract/invite/:token`). The employer can
   rewrite, add, remove or reorder any paragraph, then signs. The text is
   frozen and its SHA-256 content hash recorded.
5. **Seeker countersigns** the exact text the employer signed (the content
   hash must match).
6. **Certificate.** The executed agreement is hashed, given a certificate ID
   (`PRSP-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) and appended to the **Prosper ledger**. Both
   parties are emailed the certificate with the signed agreement attached.
   `/contract/certificate/:id` re-verifies it publicly without revealing the
   agreement text.

### The Prosper ledger

`prosperLedger` is an append-only hash chain: each block's hash covers its
height, the previous block's hash, the certificate ID, the document hash and
a timestamp, so the newest block hash fingerprints every agreement ever
recorded, and changing any past entry breaks every later link.
`GET /api/prosper-ledger` lists recent blocks and re-verifies the whole chain.

When `PROSPER_RPC_URL`, `PROSPER_REGISTRY_ADDRESS` and `PROSPER_ANCHOR_KEY`
are set, each new block is also anchored on-chain through the
`AgreementRegistry` contract in `packages/prosper-coin`, and the transaction
hash appears on the certificate.

### API

| Method | Route | Who |
|---|---|---|
| `POST` | `/api/agreements` | Seeker creates the invite |
| `GET` | `/api/agreements/:id?k=` | Seeker's tracker |
| `POST` | `/api/agreements/:id/remind` | Seeker re-emails the invite |
| `POST` | `/api/agreements/:id/sign` | Seeker countersigns |
| `GET` / `POST` | `/api/agreement-invites/:token` | Employer opens / edits & signs |
| `GET` | `/api/certificates/:certificateId` | Public verification |
| `GET` | `/api/prosper-ledger` | Public ledger |
