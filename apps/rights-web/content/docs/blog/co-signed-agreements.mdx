---
title: Invite an employer to co-sign your agreement
description: Job seekers can now invite an employer to edit, co-sign and certify an employment or contract agreement.
date: 2026-09-28
author: Rights Institute
---

The [Employment or Contract Agreement](/contract) builder now works in both
directions. A job seeker can draft an agreement and **invite the employer** to
review it, suggest edits, and co-sign.

Once both parties have signed, the agreement can be **certified**, giving each
side a verifiable record of exactly what was agreed. Each agreement also has
its own tracking page, so both parties can see where it stands.
