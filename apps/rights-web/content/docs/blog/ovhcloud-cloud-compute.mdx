The [Cloud Compute comparison](/docs/comparisons/services/cloud-compute) now
includes **OVHcloud** alongside AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, Hetzner, Cloudflare,
Vercel and DigitalOcean.

OVHcloud stands out for three things:

- **Included bandwidth.** VPS and dedicated servers come with unmetered
  traffic, and Public Cloud has no egress fees in most European and North
  American regions.
- **Anti-DDoS on every product**, at no extra cost.
- **Data sovereignty.** EU-owned infrastructure and SecNumCloud-qualified
  private cloud for regulated workloads.

The guide also adds OVHcloud as an option for budget-conscious teams that need
European data residency. Read the full write-up in the
[comparison](/docs/comparisons/services/cloud-compute).
