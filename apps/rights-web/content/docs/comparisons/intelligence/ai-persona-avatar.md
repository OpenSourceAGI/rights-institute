## How to Read the Free-Tier Metrics

### Live conversation minutes

Live minutes measure the duration of an interactive session, not the length
of a downloaded video.

Anam explicitly counts the entire session from start to finish, including
time when nobody is speaking.

A free allowance of 30 minutes with a 3-minute session cap therefore does
not permit one continuous 30-minute conversation.

### Generated-video minutes

Generated-video minutes measure produced avatar footage or exported content,
depending on the vendor.

They do not provide an equivalent number of live conversation minutes.
For example, HeyGen's three free one-minute videos are prerecorded outputs,
not three minutes of a live avatar agent.

Colossyan separates NEO and NEO2 allowances. Treat these as separate
model-specific pools rather than interchangeable minutes.

### Credits

Credits are not comparable across vendors.

| Platform and mode | Consumption | Practical interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beyond Presence: full conversational agent | 100 credits/min | 2,000 free credits cover up to 20 minutes |
| Beyond Presence: speech-to-video | 50 credits/min | The same 2,000 credits cover up to 40 minutes |
| LiveAvatar: FULL managed agent | 2 credits/min | 10 free credits cover up to 5 minutes |
| LiveAvatar: LITE avatar-only mode | 1 credit/min | The same 10 credits cover up to 10 minutes; bring your own agent stack |
| AI Studios: paid avatar-video export | 9 credits/min | Credits are shared with other features; Free also has three separate monthly exports that do not consume credits |
| Vidnoz: standard video generation | 0.5 credits/second | 60 credits would cover up to 120 seconds before other feature charges, if the advertised daily allowance applies |

> Mode equivalents are alternatives, not additive allowances.
> Beyond Presence does not provide 20 full-agent minutes plus another
> 40 rendering minutes from the same 2,000-credit balance.

## Best Starting Points

### Create a fictional person and talk to it live

Start with Anam.

Its Free plan includes text-to-avatar, image uploads, custom voice cloning,
and 30 conversation minutes each month. Upgrade before commercial use.

### Make a digital twin deliver a script

Start with HeyGen.

Its Free plan includes one custom video avatar and three short,
watermarked videos each month.

### Test Tavus before paying

Tavus provides 25 monthly conversation minutes and 5 monthly
video-generation minutes with stock replicas.

Choose Starter if your test requires included custom-replica training.

### Add a face to an existing voice-agent application

Compare Simli with LiveAvatar LITE.

Simli provides 50 free streaming minutes per month using template avatars.
LiveAvatar LITE provides up to 10 minutes from its monthly free credits.
These are rendering-oriented options; account for any separate voice,
language-model, or agent-provider charges.

### Experiment with training-video presenters

Compare Colossyan and AI Studios.

Both list custom-avatar creation on their free video plans. Check export,
watermark, and usage restrictions before building a full workflow.

## Important Caveats

- A stock avatar is not the same as creating your own custom person.
- A prerecorded presenter is not the same as a live conversational agent.
- A recurring free tier is not the same as a one-time trial.
- Session limits can matter more than the total monthly allowance.
- Avatar-only rendering prices may exclude the rest of the AI conversation stack.
- Annual-equivalent prices require an upfront annual commitment.
- Check current commercial-use, consent, and likeness requirements before publishing.
- Vidnoz and D-ID need an in-account pricing check before making a purchase decision.
