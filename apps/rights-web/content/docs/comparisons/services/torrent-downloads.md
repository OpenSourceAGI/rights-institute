## Column notes

- **Books (NYAA)** is mostly manga and artbooks, not prose. Use Anna's Archive or MyAnonaMouse for ebooks.
- **The Pirate Bay** has no native TV or anime split — its taxonomy is broad buckets (Video, Audio, Games, Apps, Porn, Other), so the movie/TV/anime columns are a split of its video category, not the site's own labels.
- **FitGirl Repacks** and **Skidrow & Reloaded** are release blogs, not catalogues. Low counts reflect curated repacks, each with its own magnets.
