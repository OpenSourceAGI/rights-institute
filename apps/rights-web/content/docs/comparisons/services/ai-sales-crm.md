## What to check before paying

I’d ask each vendor to demonstrate these on a small sample of your actual target companies:

- Can it return a founder’s current LinkedIn profile and verified work email?
- Can it inspect the company’s public documentation and explain why they need your service?
- Can you review and approve messages before sending?
- Can you export contacts, research, replies, and suppression lists?
- Does the quoted price include data credits, mailboxes, sending, and AI usage?
- What happens when it cannot verify an email or receives an opt-out?
- Can you start month-to-month rather than with a long commitment?

My proposed test: **30 companies in one niche**, with a personalized pitch about a concrete documentation problem. Evaluate the tools on accurate contacts and qualified conversations—not just the number of emails they can send.
